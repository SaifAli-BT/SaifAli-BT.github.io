// bioinformatics.toolbox

Bioinformatics Toolbox

Eight sequence-analysis tools, all running live in your browser. Nothing you paste or upload leaves this page.

Reverse Complement

Paste a DNA or RNA sequence to get its complement, reverse, and reverse complement. Ambiguity codes (N, R, Y, S, W, K, M, B, D, H, V) are supported.

GC Content & Sequence Statistics

Length, base composition, GC%, estimated molecular weight (ssDNA), and estimated melting temperature (Wallace rule for <14 nt, GC% formula for longer sequences).

ORF Finder (6 Frames)

Scans all 3 forward and 3 reverse reading frames for open reading frames (ATG → stop codon) using the standard genetic code.

DNA → Protein Translator

Translates a DNA sequence in a chosen frame (or all 6 frames) using the standard genetic code. Stop codons are shown as *.

Restriction Enzyme Mapper

Scans your sequence against 24 common Type II restriction enzymes (real recognition sites and cut offsets) and maps every cut site.

Primer Design Helper

A simplified, heuristic primer picker — not the full Primer3 thermodynamic model. It scores candidate primers near each end of your sequence by GC%, estimated Tm, repeat runs, and basic self-dimer/hairpin risk, and returns the best-ranked pairs. Use it to narrow candidates, not as a final lab decision.

Motif / Pattern Finder

Search one or more sequences (plain or multi-FASTA) for a pattern. Supports IUPAC ambiguity codes (R, Y, S, W, K, M, B, D, H, V, N) or, in regex mode, a full JavaScript regular expression.

Pairwise Sequence Aligner

Real Needleman-Wunsch (global) or Smith-Waterman (local) dynamic-programming alignment — the classic, exact algorithms, not an approximation.
